2. How much gold the UI uses

The DS sets primary to gold. 26 component styles use it, including solid buttons, links, tabs, focus states, form controls, header, hero and footer.

The brand says: avoid large gold backgrounds and avoid overusing gold in digital UI. It reserves gold for headings, dividers and accents.
